# Bounded methodology correction — 2026-10-24-methodology-v2

This version corrects named identities and interpretation. It does not establish predictive validity, optimize thresholds, change the universe, or relax financial-input/source/FX gates. Energy is outside these changes. Saved results, prices, annual sources and historical outputs are unchanged in this PR; future pipeline output records the methodology version. Historical outputs remain versioned rather than silently rewritten.

## Quant conventions

* Piotroski annual ROA and CFO share opening total assets. Prior ROA needs the actual preceding opening balance; no current/prior substitution or skipped-year comparison. Leverage includes long-term debt and its current portion, divided by average assets. Missing components stay unknown. Turnover follows Table 1 average assets; the paper prose uses opening assets, so this is an explicit convention, not a uniquely settled reading. Net Income is disclosed as a pre-extraordinary-income proxy when used. F7 uses gross reported equity cash proceeds, explicitly a proxy that cannot establish noncash issuance; neither constant shares nor net issuance proves no offer. A full count requires all nine signals; partial count is separate. LTM versus FY is labeled an overlapping interim adaptation.
* Sloan BS identity is `[(delta CA-delta cash)-(delta CL-delta current debt-delta income taxes payable)-depreciation/amortization]/average TA`. Exact named components and adjacent annual observations are required. Cash equivalents alone are not accepted as total cash plus equivalents; without that total the BS method is withheld while the independent CFO diagnostic may remain available. Overlapping LTM BS windows are withheld. The cash-flow diagnostic is `(Net Income-CFO)/average TA`, not the original Sloan earnings definition. Acquisition/discontinued-operation distortions remain possible. The existing absolute divergence threshold (>10 percentage points, strictly) is disclosed in summary and detail. Neither sign certifies durable quality.
* Adapted Ohlson coefficients and USD-millions/current-FX SIZE arithmetic remain unchanged. Output is an experimental index. The compatibility probability field is null, and no probability/risk band is displayed, even when rendering legacy saved probabilities. Original SIZE/GNP authentication remains OPEN; no replacement coefficient or scale is invented.
* Active Altman Z-double-prime is unchanged. The former book-equity hybrid is no longer called the original 1968 reference. That reference is withheld until dated market equity and a compatible currency/input contract exist. Historical Z-double-prime bands do not certify stock safety.
